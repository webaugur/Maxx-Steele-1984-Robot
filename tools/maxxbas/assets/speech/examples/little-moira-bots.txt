Little Moira lost her bots.
And does not know where to find them.
Leave them alone, and they will come home.
Wagging their antennae behind them.

She searched the bog and searched the glen.
Calling, "Come back, little metal men!"
One beeped from under a potato crate.
One whirred beside the garden gate.

"Oh bots, oh bots," said Moira Mae.
"Why ever did you run away?"
They answered back with chirps and clicks.
"We only wanted bigger circuits!"

So home they rolled on shiny feet.
Behind their mistress, neat and sweet.
And little Moira laughed with glee.
"My flock of robots, follow me!"
